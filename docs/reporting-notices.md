# Reports, CSV and notices (WP16C)

FacilityManager, Sales, Finance and OperatorAdmin can read facility metrics. The selected half-open interval is at most 366 facility-local days. Scheduled utilization uses Confirmed allocations and historical activated-lease prefixes from legacy Released allocations, current catalog unit areas, clipped retirement dates and a union of maintenance days excluded from rentable area-days; temporary holds and cancelled pre-handover bookings are excluded. Financial closure preserves the confirmed past prefix and releases only the remaining schedule; legacy released rows are clipped to termination/closure. This is a current-catalog reconstruction, not an audited historical area/publication history. Zero denominator returns null. Physical occupied area uses distinct currently uncleared units and is explicitly a current observation, not occupancy for a historical interval. Collections show currently verified receipts received during the interval (including deposits and unapplied money), recorded refunds by performed date, and a separately projected current held security liability. They do not claim all receipts are rental revenue. Held liability accounts for WP15 deductions/refunds/releases. No historical fleet or performance SLA is claimed.

Stock history CSV is available to current customer members or facility Clerk/FacilityManager/OperatorAdmin. Statement CSV is available to CustomerAdmin or Finance/OperatorAdmin. Every row is filtered by organization AND facility. Statement exports use immutable original charges plus credits/supplements, effective allocations/deductions less releases. Both export APIs record an audit before responding, use no-store/nosniff attachment download, UTF-8 BOM, invariant trusted numeric values, quoted text and apostrophe escaping for formula/whitespace/control candidates. At most 5000 rows; overflow refuses the whole export, requiring a narrower interval. Spreadsheet re-import/transformation can change escaping; inspect files before removing text prefixes.

Notice generation and dispatch are separate explicit local job commands. No scheduler, email or external provider is started. FacilityManager/OperatorAdmin queues up to 100 approaching-end leases (next seven days) and 100 submitted/approved stock requests per source page. Repeated sweeps are safe: stable business keys distinguish lease end dates and request states. Page sweeps must restart at page1 to pick up concurrent candidates. Dispatch drains oldest pending100 without offset, under a facility transaction lock; the notice and delivered marker commit together. Unique business/outbox keys prevent duplicate notices on retries or concurrent dispatch. A failed transaction is retryable; queued is not delivered. Persisted notices are historical events, not proof a request remains actionable. Lists recheck current organization/facility scope and paginate50.

Required command identity/body/context survive uncertain UI results including retries rejected after authority withdrawal; the UI does not create a replacement key until completion is confirmed. Browser/manual acceptance remains deferred. Production scheduling, cleanup/retention, volume/latency and proxy abuse controls require separate operational review.
