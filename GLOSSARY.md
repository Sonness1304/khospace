# WarehouseRent business language

One warehouse operator leases physical space to customer organizations and records custody movements of customer-owned goods.

## Space and rental

**Operator**: The single business running the facilities and providing storage-space rental.
_Avoid_: Marketplace, customer tenant.

**Facility**: A physical warehouse site with an address, operating hours and a collection of rentable units.
_Avoid_: Stock item, account.

**Unit**: A fixed, independently leasable physical area with a published floor area and boundaries. It is rented as a whole even when the customer uses less than its area.
_Avoid_: Arbitrary requested m², SKU, pallet slot.

**Customer organization**: The person or company renting space and owning the goods stored there.
_Avoid_: Operator, login account.

**Membership**: A user's authorized relationship with a customer organization.
_Avoid_: Lease, physical occupancy.

**Quote**: A time-limited price proposal for specified units and dates. It is not a capacity reservation.
_Avoid_: Confirmed booking, payment.

**Hold**: A temporary exclusive allocation of specified units for specified dates pending a confirmation decision.
_Avoid_: Quote, permanent lease.

**Booking**: A recorded rental request whose confirmed allocation can become a lease.
_Avoid_: Stock reservation, physical check-in.

**Lease**: The approved rental arrangement describing the customer, units, dates and agreed commercial terms.
_Avoid_: Executed legal contract, invoice, booking payment.

**Occupancy**: The physical use of a unit, established by handover and clearance records.
_Avoid_: Scheduled availability, paid status.

## Goods and money

**Customer item**: A customer-specific goods identifier and description, with a defined base quantity unit.
_Avoid_: Rentable unit, operator-owned merchandise.

**Stock document**: A controlled request/record for receipt, issue or transfer of goods.
_Avoid_: Lease, invoice.

**Stock posting**: The committed movement of a quantity into, out of, or between locations for one customer.
_Avoid_: Direct balance edit, draft request.

**Stock ledger**: The chronological record of posted movements and linked reversals from which balances can be reconciled.
_Avoid_: Mutable total field, financial general ledger.

**Deposit**: Refundable security money tracked separately from rental charges.
_Avoid_: Revenue, paid invoice.

**Charge statement**: An application record of rental/service charges, credits and allocations for a period.
_Avoid_: Statutory tax invoice.

**Payment record**: An operator-recorded receipt of money supported by a reference, not proof generated by a customer screenshot.
_Avoid_: Payment gateway settlement, accepted deposit merely from upload.
